TWO-LINK ARM: SELF-STUDY WORKSHEET
Model: ideal planar arm. L1=L2=1 m. Angles in degrees, counterclockwise.
q1 is measured from +x. q2 is relative to link 1, not the world.

1. Predict x,y before running each pose: (0,0), (90,0), (0,90), (45,-90).
2. Compare your calculation with the model. Record any discrepancy.
3. Reach target (1,1). Find two different pairs of joint angles.
4. Try target (2,2). Explain why it cannot be reached.
5. Extension: change both link lengths to 0.5 m in a local copy of the model.
   Predict the new maximum reach, then check your prediction.
6. Explain two things this model leaves out about a real robot.

Evidence: export one CSV for a successful target pose; keep your predictions,
calculations, alternate solution and explanation of the unreachable target.
Self-study only: no submission, grading or course credit is provided here.
